# Product Vision

## BlueprintForge AI

BlueprintForge AI is an open-source AI-native platform for turning raw app ideas into protected blueprints, builder build packs, company launch partner bids, and revenue-share app opportunities.

---

## Core idea

The platform helps creators move from raw idea to build-ready clarity.

It combines:

- AI Product Architect documentation generation
- compact object-based product documentation
- protected blueprint layers
- builder build packs
- company launch partner bidding
- 9-day bid windows
- bid recoupment
- 40/30/30 revenue participation
- competitive intelligence
- access logging and evidence support

---

## Main users

### Creator / Admin

Creates app concepts, manages blueprints, approves builders, launches company campaigns, controls access, and tracks revenue.

### Builder / Vibecoder

Builds approved app concepts using builder-ready documentation and may receive revenue participation after company bid recoupment.

### Company Launch Partner

Company inside a target niche that can bid to become the exclusive launch partner for an app opportunity.

### AI System

Turns messy ideas into structured product architecture, validates documentation, and generates builder handoff packs.

---

## Core flow

1. Creator enters a raw app idea.
2. AI Product Architect extracts product structure.
3. System generates compact object documentation.
4. Sensitive blueprint content is split into protected layers.
5. Builders apply or contribute.
6. Selected companies receive confidential invites.
7. Companies bid during a 9-day window.
8. Highest qualified bidder becomes launch partner.
9. Builder receives Builder Build Pack.
10. App is built and launched.
11. Company recoups winning bid from future net revenue.
12. After recoupment, revenue is split: Creator 40%, Builder 30%, Company 30%.

---

## What makes this different

BlueprintForge AI is not a generic idea chatbot.

It is designed to become a structured product architecture engine that can repeatedly turn ideas into build-ready app opportunities.

The key differentiator is the **Builder Build Pack**: builders should receive clear implementation documentation, not vague app ideas.

---

## Product principle

Every output should help move from idea to build-ready clarity.

If a feature does not improve documentation quality, builder execution, protection, company launch partner commitment, or revenue tracking, it should be questioned.
