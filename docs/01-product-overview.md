# Product Overview

BlueprintForge AI turns raw ideas into structured, build-ready app blueprints.

## Two systems

- Blueprint Marketplace
- AI Product Architect

## Flow

1. Idea
2. Structure
3. Documentation
4. Company bid
5. Builder execution

## Revenue

Company pays upfront, recoups first, then split:
- Creator 40%
- Builder 30%
- Company 30%

## Core idea

The system proves that strong product thinking can be executed by builders through clear documentation.
