## Core idea

The goal is to create a system where:

1. creators generate app ideas;
2. AI turns those ideas into structured documentation;
3. builders receive build-ready packs;
4. companies can bid to become launch partners;
5. revenue can be tracked through bid recoupment and 40/30/30 participation.

---

## Open-source boundary

The platform itself is open source.

Future niche app blueprints, real company lists, private business data, real bid data, and confidential opportunities are not automatically open source.
