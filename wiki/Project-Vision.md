# Project Vision

BlueprintForge AI is an open-source AI-native platform for turning raw app ideas into protected blueprints, builder build packs, company launch partner bids, and revenue-share app opportunities.

---

## What the platform should do

BlueprintForge AI should help creators move from raw idea to structured product opportunity.

The app should support:

- idea intake
- AI product architecture
- compact object documentation
- builder-ready handoff packs
- protected blueprint layers
- creator and builder profiles
- company launch partner campaigns
- 9-day bid windows
- bid recoupment tracking
- 40/30/30 revenue participation dashboards

---

## Why it matters

Ideas alone are fragile.

Builders need clear documentation.
Companies need a reason to commit.
Creators need protection and upside.
AI coding agents need precise specs.

BlueprintForge AI exists to create the system around that.

---

## Product principle

Every feature should help move from:

> idea → structure → protection → funding → build → revenue participation

If a feature does not improve clarity, builder execution, protection, company commitment, or collaboration quality, it should be questioned.
