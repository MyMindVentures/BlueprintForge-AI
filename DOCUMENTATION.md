# 1. App Overview

## **[APP-001] Blueprint Marketplace + AI Product Architect PWA**

Blueprint Marketplace + AI Product Architect PWA is a protected app opportunity platform where a creator turns raw app ideas into structured, AI-layered, build-ready app blueprints for specific niches. The platform combines two systems into one product:

1. **Blueprint Marketplace** — a protected business model where niche companies bid to become exclusive company launch partners for app opportunities.
2. **AI Product Architect** — an internal product documentation engine that turns messy ideas, brainstorms, notes, and concept conversations into structured, object-based product documentation for builders, developers, AI coding agents, and launch partners.

The creator can brainstorm app concepts through an Admin AI Chat or input interface. The AI Product Architect engine extracts the product meaning, identifies roles, screens, capabilities, user flows, AI systems, business rules, monetization logic, access rules, data models, and build phases. It then generates clean object-based documentation that can be safely split into protected layers.

The main commercial model is built around a **Company Launch Partner**. The creator develops a confidential app opportunity for a niche and sends a safe opportunity invite to selected companies. These companies can accept confidentiality terms, view a protected teaser, and place a bid during a limited 9-day bid window. The **highest qualified bidder** becomes the exclusive launch partner. The winning company pays the accepted bid upfront. Once the app generates net revenue, the company first recovers that bid. After full recoupment, future net revenue is split: **Creator 40%, Builder 30%, Company Launch Partner 30%**.

The builder is the technical execution partner. Because the builder needs excellent documentation to build fast and correctly, every approved build receives a dedicated **Builder Build Pack** generated by the AI Product Architect engine. This build pack includes object-based product documentation, technical implementation instructions, component maps, data model drafts, API route suggestions, acceptance criteria, build phases, and AI coding agent prompts.

The result is a full app opportunity system: the creator generates valuable app concepts, companies fund and validate the opportunity, builders receive perfect documentation to execute, and all parties collaborate through protected access, agreements, audit logs, and revenue participation.

# 5. Monetization / Business Model

## **[MON-001] Company Launch Partner Winning Bid**

Selected companies inside a niche place bids during a 9-day window. The highest qualified bidder becomes the exclusive launch partner and pays the accepted bid upfront.

## **[MON-002] Company Bid Recoupment**

Future net revenue first goes toward recouping the winning company’s accepted upfront bid.

## **[MON-003] B2B 40/30/30 Revenue Share**

After full company bid recoupment:

* Creator: 40%
* Builder: 30%
* Company Launch Partner: 30%

## **[MON-004] Builder Build Pack Value**

The builder receives excellent structured documentation, reducing build ambiguity and increasing execution quality. This is a core value driver of the marketplace.

## **[MON-005] Future AI Product Architect SaaS Licensing**

The internal documentation engine can later become a standalone SaaS with Free, Pro, Team, and Agency plans.

## **[MON-006] Future Platform Commission / Multi-Creator Expansion**

Optional future model if the platform expands to multiple creators, agencies, or marketplace-wide revenue-share transactions.

# 8. Global Rules

## Builder Documentation Rules

* Builder must receive build-ready documentation, not vague idea notes.
* Builder Build Pack must include object documentation, implementation sequence, component map, data model draft, API outline, acceptance criteria, and coding-agent prompt.
* Handoff must reference confirmed object IDs.
* Uncertain assumptions must be labeled clearly.
* Locked sections cannot be overwritten by regeneration.

## Protection Rules

* Public cards must never show full features, userflows, monetization mechanics, growth loops, or internal strategy.
* Protected layers require server-side authorization.
* Protected content views must be watermarked and logged.
* Unauthorized API responses must never include protected content.

## Company Bidding Rules

* Company campaigns are invite-only.
* Company must accept confidentiality before teaser access.
* Default bid window is 9 calendar days.
* Winner is selected as highest qualified bid, not automatically highest amount.
* Bid quality can include bid amount, niche fit, distribution potential, brand credibility, and launch readiness.

## Revenue Rules

* The old creator/builder 50/50 split is not the default model.
* Winning company pays upfront bid.
* Winning company recoups bid first from future net revenue.
* After recoupment: Creator 40%, Builder 30%, Company 30%.
* Revenue is not guaranteed.
* Recoupment is not guaranteed unless sufficient net revenue exists.

## AI Product Architect Rules

* Raw input must be preserved.
* Generated documentation must be versioned.
* Every capability should map to an object, screen, workflow, or module.
* Every AI feature must support a real product function.
* The system must distinguish confirmed logic from suggested improvements.
* Validation suggestions must not silently replace confirmed documentation.

## Legal Wording Rules

Use:

* Exclusive Launch Partner
* Revenue Participation Partner
* Commercial launch partner payment
* Bid recoupment
* Evidence support
* Agreement-backed access
* Based on checked sources
* No full-concept match found

Avoid unless legally structured:

* Shareholder
* Investor
* Securities
* Guaranteed ROI
* Guaranteed legal proof
* This app definitely does not exist
* We will sue automatically

## Mobile & Tablet Rules

* All key flows must be smartphone and tablet optimized.
* No horizontal overflow.
* Tables should become cards on mobile.
* Bid room, agreement gate, onboarding, build pack, canvas editor, and dashboards must remain usable on mobile.
* Touch targets should be at least 44px.
