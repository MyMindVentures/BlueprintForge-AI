# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

BlueprintForge AI is an open-source builder challenge and collaboration project.

We want this community to be welcoming, respectful, ambitious, and builder-friendly.

Contributors should be able to share ideas, submit pull requests, discuss architecture, and improve the project without harassment, personal attacks, or disrespect.

---

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give clear feedback.
- Explain tradeoffs.
- Welcome new contributors.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Use safe mock data.
- Respect protected-information boundaries.

---

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or insults.
- Personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Posting secrets or credentials.
- Sharing confidential app blueprints or private company data.
- Spamming issues or pull requests.
- Misrepresenting contribution rights or revenue-share rights.

---

## Important collaboration boundary

Contributing to this open-source project does not automatically grant access to private app opportunities, confidential documentation, private company campaigns, revenue-share projects, or protected business data.

Trust is built through contribution quality, communication, and consistency.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from the project.
