# Code of Conduct

BlueprintForge AI is a community for Architects, Builders, creators, neurodiverse thinkers, designers, developers, AI engineers, and contributors who want to turn ideas into living products.

We want this project to be ambitious and safe at the same time.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent;
- give clear feedback without personal attacks;
- welcome beginners and non-traditional builders;
- respect neurodiverse communication styles;
- keep discussions focused on the work;
- protect confidential or sensitive information;
- avoid exploitative or manipulative behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or intimidation;
- discrimination or exclusionary behavior;
- mocking people for being non-coders, neurodiverse, new to open source, or learning in public;
- sharing private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors into unpaid work outside the open-source scope;
- making false financial, legal, or ownership claims;
- attempting to access protected blueprint layers without permission.

## Collaboration principle

BlueprintForge AI is built around complementary strengths.

Some people are Architects.
Some people are Builders.
Some people are both.

No role is treated as inferior.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that goes against this code of conduct, open a maintainer discussion or contact the repository owner through the public GitHub profile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict access when behavior harms the project or community.
